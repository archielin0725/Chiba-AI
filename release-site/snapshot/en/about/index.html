About CHIBA Taiwan

Founded in 1853, German glovemaking specialist Chiba Gloves Germany is world-renowned for ergonomic tailoring, high-performance breathable fabrics, and vibration damping technology. Chiba Taiwan Co., Ltd. serves as the official exclusive distributor in Taiwan, presenting the complete authentic lineup across professional sports and protective applications. Core product lines include Cycling Gloves, Strength Training & Fitness, Outdoor & Skiing, and Professional Industrial Safety.
